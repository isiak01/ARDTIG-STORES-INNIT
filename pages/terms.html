Payments and proof

Before sending money, confirm the displayed price and payment recipient. Pay the exact amount shown and upload an unaltered proof of payment. A payment request remains pending until reviewed. Do not send money to anyone claiming to represent the store outside the payment details shown on the payment page.

Digital goods and refunds

Digital goods may be difficult or impossible to recover after delivery. Once a digital item or account has been delivered, refunds are not offered for an incorrect recipient, incorrect UID, changed mind, or payment made in the wrong amount. Contact the store before paying if any detail is unclear. This does not remove rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Scams and account safety

Never share your password, verification code, or payment credentials in chat. ARDTIG STORES will not ask for your Google password or a one-time sign-in code. Report suspicious messages and use only the official store website.

Marketplace availability

Listings, prices, and availability may change. A listing is not reserved until a payment request has been approved. We may reject incomplete, altered, or unverifiable payment proof.

Contact
